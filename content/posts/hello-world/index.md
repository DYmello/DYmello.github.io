This site is a home for research notes, technical articles, experiments, paper reading, and ongoing questions in artificial intelligence.

## What to expect

Topics will include machine learning, large language models, autonomous agents, alignment, trustworthy AI, and the practical details of research work.

Mathematical notation is supported for technical writing. For example, the predictive entropy of a model can be written as

\[
H(Y \mid x) = -\sum_y p(y \mid x) \log p(y \mid x).
\]

The goal is to keep each entry clear, reproducible, and useful to future work.
